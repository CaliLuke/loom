Two independent pre-existing view owners were exposed and tracked rather than
claimed fixed here. [#926](https://github.com/CaliLuke/loom/issues/926) concerns

of the views package. Named scalar controls here use ordinary results/errors;
projected native scalar controls remain covered. Neither correction belongs to
